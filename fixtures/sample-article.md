# The Quiet Hour Before Dawn

by Guillermo Siliceo

There is a particular stillness that settles over a city in the hour before
sunrise. The streetlights still burn, but their glow has begun to lose the
argument with the sky. Somewhere a delivery truck idles. A baker, unseen,
is already three hours into the day.

## Why the early hour matters

Researchers who study circadian rhythms note that the pre-dawn window is when
the body's core temperature reaches its lowest point. It is, paradoxically,
both the hardest time to wake and the clearest time to think once you have.

## A small ritual

Brew something warm. Open a window. Let the cold air remind you that the world
is large and indifferent and, for this one quiet hour, entirely yours.

The sun will arrive soon enough, and with it the noise.
